OUR STORY

Rooted in Jaipur.
Open to the world.

PetPooja began with a simple thought: a meal can become a memory. Since opening our doors, we've built a restaurant around generous hospitality, good ingredients, and the joy of lingering over one more course.

Our kitchen follows the seasons and works with trusted local producers. Familiar flavours meet a little curiosity, and every plate is made to be enjoyed together.

Whether it's a first date, a family gathering or just a Tuesday worth celebrating, we're glad you're here.
